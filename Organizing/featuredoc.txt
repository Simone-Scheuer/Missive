## 3.1 Personas

# Stelio Kontos

- Stelio Kontos is a 28-year-old board game manager in Seattle, Washington.
- He is the primary community organizer for events at the store and also participates actively in them.
- His main hobbies are playing tabletop roleplaying games and board games, and he also enjoys practicing drawing and occasionally attending LARP events.
- He particularly enjoys the technical (crunchy) aspects of these hobbies.
- As the manager of the store, he regularly communicates with his employees and community members via the work and community Discord servers.
- He is highly comfortable with technology and is used to navigating complex online gaming systems.
- He typically engages in short sessions of 1–2 message exchanges, often for affirmational or comedic purposes.
- His main goal in using *Missive* is to enhance community engagement and maintain immersion during games.
- He is frustrated by products that disrupt immersion or make coordinating community interactions difficult.
- To work around these frustrations, he uses *Missive*’s flexible messaging and drawing features to send quick, context-sensitive notes.
- He particularly enjoys using *Missive*’s drawing tools to send fun, customized messages to employees, store regulars, and friends, and to send secret messages to players while dungeon mastering.
- This allows him to facilitate engagement, inject humor, and maintain the immersive experience of the game while keeping everyone connected.
- TLDR: social butterlfy community manager likes to use it to send funny compliments to employees and  messages for enhancing tabletop game immersion.
